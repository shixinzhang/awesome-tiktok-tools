## Transcription & Captions

- [TokTranscript](https://toktranscript.com/?utm_source=github_awesome&utm_campaign=awesome_list) - AI TikTok transcription with viral breakdowns and 100+ language translation.
- [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/) - Auto-captions and short video editing for creators.
- [Captions](https://www.captions.ai/) - AI-powered captions and subtitles for short-form video.
- [VEED.io](https://www.veed.io/) - Browser-based video editor with automatic subtitle generation.
- [Riverside Transcription](https://riverside.fm/transcription) - Transcription with speaker labels for podcasters and creators.
- [AssemblyAI](https://www.assemblyai.com/) - Speech-to-text API with speaker diarization and content moderation.
- [Rev](https://www.rev.com/) - AI and human transcription, captions, and subtitles.
- [Notta](https://www.notta.ai/) - Real-time transcription across browser, mobile, and meeting tools.

## Editing & Subtitles

- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/) - TikTok's first-party video editor for desktop, mobile, and web.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/) - Edit video by editing the transcript, plus AI overdub.
- [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/) - Collaborative online editor with auto-captions and subtitles.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Drag-and-drop video templates sized for TikTok and Reels.
- [Adobe Premiere Rush](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere-rush.html) - Mobile-first cut of Adobe Premiere Pro for short-form video.
- [Klap](https://klap.app/) - Turn long videos into TikTok-ready clips with auto-reframe and captions.
- [Opus Clip](https://www.opus.pro/) - AI clipping tool that extracts viral moments from long videos.

## Analytics & Insights

- [Exolyt](https://exolyt.com/) - TikTok analytics with profile, hashtag, and competitor benchmarks.
- [Pentos](https://pentos.co/) - Profile, hashtag, and song tracking for TikTok and Instagram.
- [Tokchart](https://tokchart.com/) - TikTok creator and hashtag charts with historical data.
- [Emplifi](https://emplifi.io/) - Enterprise social media analytics covering TikTok and others.
- [Meltwater](https://meltwater.com/) - Media and social listening with TikTok coverage.
- [Brandwatch](https://www.brandwatch.com/) - Social listening and consumer intelligence including TikTok.

## Hashtag & Keyword Research

- [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) - Official TikTok hashtag, song, and creative inspiration explorer.
- [Display Purposes](https://displaypurposes.com/) - Hashtag recommendations with related-tag clustering.
- [RiteTag](https://ritetag.com/) - Live hashtag suggestions ranked by engagement potential.
- [Best Hashtags](https://best-hashtags.com/) - Free hashtag generator across niches and platforms.

## API & Developer Tools

- [TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/) - Official TikTok API platform including Display API and Login Kit.
- [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) - Command-line downloader supporting TikTok and hundreds of other sites.
- [TikAPI](https://tikapi.io/) - Unofficial REST API for TikTok user, video, and search data.
- [Apify TikTok Scraper](https://www.apify.com/clockworks/tiktok-scraper) - Hosted scraper for TikTok profiles, hashtags, and videos.
- [TikTok-Api (davidteather)](https://github.com/davidteather/TikTok-Api) - Python wrapper for the unofficial TikTok API.
