# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported to [conduct@toktranscript.com](mailto:conduct@toktranscript.com).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
acceptable standards of behavior and will take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

For the full text, see <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
